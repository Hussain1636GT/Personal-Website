CAD

During my studies i was the CAD design engineer for a robot fighting group project. A position I has to write cv, cover letter and do a 10 minute interview with the two group supervisors. All of the groups were supposed to have two CAD design engineers but due to the other CAD engineer droping out a week into the role i had to complete the role myself.

The shape of the design was important so that the robot could have a good attacking quality. This was a highlighted through the ramp which was inputted on to the design. The ramp would be able to deflect incoming attacks from different robots hence it was essential for the robot to have this design quality which allowed the robot to protect its self.

It can be assumed that the manufacturing of the robot would be very simplistic due to the simple shapes which the robot has included. As it can be seen the final model has rectangle shapes angled at different angles to make the ramp of the robot. As well as this, it has coned shaped spikes to make the weapon.

During the designing, modeling, creting of engineering drawing and manufacturing i staying in constant comunication with the mancufactures so that anything i had overlooked could be modified or redesigned and new engineering drawings created.
